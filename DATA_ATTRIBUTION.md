# Source-data attribution

The BridgeCheck candidate bank is derived from **“CABO 2018-2019 Leaf-Level Spectra v2”** by Shan
Kothari, Rosalie Beauchamp-Rioux, Florence Blanchard, Anna L. Crofts, Alizée Girard, Xavier
Guilbeault-Mayers, Paul W. Hacker, Juliana Pardo, Anna K. Schweiger, Sabrina Demers-Thibeault, Anne
Bruneau, Nicholas C. Coops, Margaret Kalacska, Mark Vellend, and Etienne Laliberté; published by
EcoSIS; DOI [`10.21232/58kApoRy`](https://doi.org/10.21232/58kApoRy).

Source: <https://data.ecosis.org/dataset/a12286ac-929f-4e3e-9b9c-517e10fe4087>

Changes made by AlphaSpectra: restricted the source to the frozen EXP-0123 training partition;
quarantined invalid or incomplete rows; converted EWT, LMA, chlorophyll and carotenoid units;
computed coordinate-wise medians by biological group; omitted source identities and geolocation;
rendered candidate spectra using `prospect`/PROSPECT-D 2.0.0; and applied the frozen global affine
calibration. This is a transformed numerical candidate bank, not the original CABO dataset.
